| 2026-08-02 | **Lecture 25 authored end-to-end in kit format (S1→S4) — the acceptance test set at repo creation, and it passes on both targets.** Donor lecture 25 re-authored from its 13-page handwritten source into `content/lecture25.tex`: 8 sections / 20 content frames covering the honeycomb lattice, the enforced symmetries and the space group `p6/mmm1'`, the reciprocal lattice + TRIMs, the band representation (`B(I)`, `B(C_6)`, `B(\TRS)`), the nearest-neighbour model, and the graphene Dirac point; plus 5 TikZ figures with `alt/lecture25.tex`, and `announcements/lecture25.tex` (verbatim, slides-only). **Gates:** `build 25` PASS — math 206, unparsed 2 (~1%), figures 5, bare-math-text 0, pa11y clean ×8 pages, zero `[ALT MISSING]`; `slides 25` PASS — 28 pages incl. overlays; zero overfull boxes on either target. **Decisions, all now in `conventions.md`:** (1) **bordered/labelled matrices REJECTED on accessibility grounds** — `\bordermatrix`'s floating row/column labels are visual-spatial information that reads badly or not at all for a screen-reader user, *and* they drop the formula out of parsed MathML (measured: unparsed 2→6 when used, back to 2 when removed); house rule is a plain `pmatrix` with the basis named in the introducing sentence ("in the sublattice basis $(A,B)$, …"), which serves every reader identically. (2) Boxed board callouts are emphasis → `\alert`, never a titled `block` — inventing a title to justify a block breaches prose fidelity. (3) Write `\otimes` explicitly even where the source juxtaposes factors. (4) New **Notation (physics)** section: Pauli *superscripts*, `\TRS`/`\conj`, `\sg{...}` (lecture-local in 25; promote to `shared/macros.tex` as `\providecommand` when lecture 26 wants it), a mirror's subscript names its plane *normal* (keep the Hermann–Mauguin index — students look the group up on the Bilbao server), "irreps". (5) New **Frame structure** section: `\announcementsframe` after the title frame; every lecture ends with a *static* Takeaways frame. (6) **Alt text is richly descriptive and carries what the figure *argues***, not just what it depicts; conversely, board thinking-marks (traversal arrows, lassos) are *not* reproduced — reproduce what the drawing says, not every pen stroke. (7) Two figures showing the same path must label it identically; symmetry-equivalent representatives are a free choice, which is exactly why the choice has to agree across figures. **Deviations from the source, all instructor-directed and deliberate:** the p10 inversion constraint `⟨Bσ −R|H|AσR⟩ = t` is **wrong** (a board slip) and is transcribed *corrected* as `⟨AσR|H|BσR⟩ = t`, with one connecting line of derivation; the p2 bottom 4-band Wilson pair dropped; the p12 Brillouin-zone-path inset folded into the band-structure figure (6 figures → 5), whose horizontal axis now *is* that path; frame 8 re-laid with `columns` — matching the source page's own left/right layout — rather than split. Progressive reveals added to the three heavy derivation frames only; intro/recap/Takeaways left static. **Two kit findings, both added to `docs/troubleshooting.md`:** a bare palette colour in a TikZ node's option list silently overrides a `fill=white` coming from the node style (because `color=` sets the fill too), so white-backed figure labels render as solid coloured boxes — colour text with `text=<colour>`; and TikZ `scale=` does **not** scale text nodes, so shrinking a figure to fit a frame is not a free density fix (re-lay the frame instead). Resumable stage record: `notes-lecture25.md`. |
